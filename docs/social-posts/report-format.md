Your ER diagram must match your database.

Most report problems are not English problems. They are structure problems.

Students put screenshots in random chapters. They write “System Design” without the actual architecture. They paste an ER diagram from a template, but the database has different table names and fields.

Panel members notice this fast.

I wrote a free guide for the final year project report format, chapter by chapter. It covers what to put in all 8 chapters: introduction, literature survey, requirement analysis, system design, implementation, testing, results, and conclusion.

It also lists the diagrams commonly required: use case, DFD, ER diagram, architecture diagram, flowchart, module diagram, and database schema where applicable.

One viva question to prepare for:
“Show the table where this ER relationship is implemented.”

If your ER diagram and database do not match, that answer becomes messy.
