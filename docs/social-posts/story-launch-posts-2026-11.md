and change any detail that is not true to you.** Post as yourself, one at a time, not all in one day.

## Rules that keep these from being spam

- One link per post, and the post must make sense without clicking.
- The linkedin-bot already posts Tue, Wed and Thu at 10:00 UTC. Post these by hand on **Mon, Fri or Sat** so they
  do not collide with it.
- Reddit and student groups: read the group's self-promotion rule first. If it bans links, answer the question
  in the comments without a link, and add the link only when someone asks. Never post the same text in two groups.
- Maximum two manual posts a week, which matches the site's publishing pace.
