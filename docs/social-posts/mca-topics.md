Your topic also decides your viva.

Many MCA students choose a final year project title because it sounds modern. Then approval happens, development starts, and the viva question becomes the real problem.

I wrote a free guide with 20 MCA final year project topics across AI, full-stack, real-time systems, computer vision, and mobile apps.

Each topic includes the stack and an honest difficulty rating. More importantly, each topic lists the viva question it is likely to attract.

For example, a face recognition attendance system is not just “Python and OpenCV”. You may have to explain embeddings, false positives, model accuracy, lighting issues, and why simple image matching is not enough.

A real-time chat app may sound simpler, but you should be ready to explain WebSockets, message delivery, database structure, and online status.

Use this before you submit your topic for approval. Choose the question you are ready to answer.
