# Staffing consultancies and talent networks — global list

Compiled 2026-10-21. Every firm here is **employer-paid** — the client company
pays the fee, never the candidate.

**Every URL below was verified by fetching the site.** Where a page published
only a relative path, the full URL is that path on the firm's own domain. Where
a site could not be reached, it appears in §8 with the reason instead of a
guessed URL — a wrong domain for a recruitment firm is how people end up on
phishing sites, so nothing here is inferred.

Email addresses appear only where the firm actually publishes one. Most
recruitment firms deliberately publish no candidate inbox and route through a CV
upload instead, so the submission lands in their ATS. That is the database their
recruiters search when a mandate comes in, so the upload is the intended channel,
not a worse one.

## 7. How to actually use this list

The upload alone rarely produces anything. The sequence matters.

### Step 1 — register in the ATS

Work down §1 first, then your region. Budget an evening. This is not the
outreach — it is so that when a recruiter searches their database for "React Node
senior", you are a result. Skip it and a cold email gets a "please upload your
CV" reply, which is the one outcome worth avoiding.

### Step 2 — email a named recruiter

This is the step that produces interviews.

- Search LinkedIn for the firm name plus your stack, e.g. Michael Page recruiter React
- Pick someone who **posted a full-stack role in the last 30 days**. They have a
  live mandate and a quota.
- Get their address from the **Contact info** panel on their profile, or from the
  job post itself — recruiters put their email in posts constantly.
- Draft with the **"A staffing consultant"** mode at `/outreach`, which builds a
  short note from the portfolio and links the résumé PDF.

One email to a person who places your stack beats fifty to a generic inbox.

### Step 3 — follow up once

Five working days later. One follow-up, then move to the next firm.

### Priority order

1. **§1, all ten.** Highest signal, fastest, USD rates. Andela, Uplers and
   Flexiple have exact signup URLs — start there.
2. **§2 India** — most open MERN mandates for your location.
3. **§3 Gulf** — Halian and TASC first; both are IT-specialist.
4. **§4–6** — slower and more gatekept, but the route to a permanent role at a
   large company.

Register with ten to fifteen in total. Two or three will produce everything, and
there is no way to know in advance which.

## 9. Two things to watch everywhere

**Never pay a fee.** Michael Page, Adecco, CIEL, Quess, Nityo, Charterhouse,
Black Pearl, Randstad and Morgan McKinley all publish scam warnings about people
impersonating their recruiters and asking candidates for money. A genuine
employer-paid consultancy never invoices the candidate. Adecco states outright
that it charges candidates nothing; Charterhouse states it will never ask for a
deposit to secure a job; Quess runs `alert@quesscorp.com` purely for reports.

**Check the sender domain.** Mail from these firms comes from their own domain —
`@adecco.com`, `@cielhr.com`, `@manpower-me.com` are the ones stated explicitly
on their own sites. An offer from a Gmail address claiming to be a staffing firm
is a scam, and several of these firms say so themselves. Michael Page UAE
specifically warns about impersonation over WhatsApp and Telegram.
