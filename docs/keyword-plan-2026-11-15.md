# Keyword plan: what each site should rank for (15 Nov 2026)

Why: Search Console showed the portfolio ranking for CRM, delivery-app and billing terms (old service topics), nothing for hiring
intent. This plan picks target keywords from real demand and from what actually ranks, then maps each to a page.

Method and spend (about $0.23 of treg): Serpstat volumes (thin coverage, India and US), DataForSEO Google Ads volumes for 139 keywords
in India and the US (Google Ads data, $0.09 a call), Google SERP for 22 shortlisted keywords (top 10 domains, to judge who a
low-authority site can beat). SE Ranking's own account was out of credit, so it was not used. Volumes are monthly searches.
