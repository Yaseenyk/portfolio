# HOD / placement cell outreach

One action that pays twice: a department that links to the free question bank
gives you an educational backlink (the kind that actually moves a young domain),
and the same conversation is the `/colleges` sales motion.

**Lead with the free resource, not the service.** A cold email selling project
mentorship gets deleted. A cold email offering something a department can hand
to students today gets a reply, and the service is one click away on the page
you linked.

## Who to contact

In priority order:

1. **HOD, Computer Science / IT / MCA** — decides what gets recommended to a batch
2. **Training & Placement Officer** — usually the most responsive, and the one
   who runs workshops
3. **Final year project coordinator** — where one exists, this is the real
   decision-maker
4. **Department library / resources page owner** — the person who actually adds
   the link

Find addresses on the college's department page. Use their institutional
address, never a personal one. Send individually — never CC or BCC a list; a
visible recipient list guarantees deletion and can get you blocked.

**Volume:** 20 in the first batch. Personalise the first line of each. If you
cannot personalise, do not send it.

**Why this works:** it gives before it asks, the link request is a soft aside
rather than the point, and the credential is verifiable in one click. Nothing
is claimed that the recipient cannot check.

## Email 3 — the workshop, only after a warm reply

Do not send this cold. It goes only to someone who has already replied
positively.

> **Subject:** A session for your final year batch, no cost
>

>
> Thank you for the reply — glad it is useful.

## Rules

- **Never** claim past students, placements, results, or testimonials. You have
  none yet, and one fabricated claim to an academic ends the channel permanently.
- Do not attach anything. Attachments from unknown senders get filtered.
- Plain text. HTML templates read as marketing.
- Do not ask for a link in email 1. If the resource is good, some will add it
  unprompted, and those are the links worth having.
- Track who you contacted and when, so nobody gets emailed twice.

## What success looks like

Realistically, from 20 emails: 3–5 replies, 1–2 links, 1 workshop. That is a
good outcome. Educational domains carry real weight, so even two links move a
young domain more than months of publishing.
