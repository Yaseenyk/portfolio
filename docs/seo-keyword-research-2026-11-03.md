### Where SpotAI is lacking

1. **No page owns "gym management software".** gymforce.in ranks 5 with 42 keywords and DA 9. Our
   India guide targets "gym management software india" (KD 52); the broader 1,300 term needs the
   owner landing page `/for-gym-owners/` retitled and led with that phrase.
2. **No free tool.** okfit.in ranks 47 keywords almost entirely from calculators (1RM at 5,400 per
   variant). SpotAI has the AI coach but no stand-alone utility page. A 1RM calculator, a gym
   membership fee calculator (mygymdesk wins "gym membership fees" at 10 with KD 6), or a
   BMR/calorie calculator for Indian food would be the cheapest link-earning pages.
3. **No device or integration pages.** gymforce ranks 15 to 30 for ESSL biometric model names at KD 0.
   SpotAI's face check-in is "optional and comes later", so only write about this if the product
   supports it; do not claim it.
4. **"AI form check" is open.** formchecker.ai ranks 1 keyword and sensai.fit has a brand pull. No
   guide page of ours targets "ai form check" or "ai squat form checker" directly, but the volumes
   are probably tiny (I did not seed that phrase, so it is unmeasured). Treat as a positioning page, not a traffic
   source.
5. **Zero own backlinks.** Competitors with 15 to 47 keywords carry 48 to 166 referring domains.
   Directory listings (Capterra India, gym software lists, Indian startup directories) are where they
   come from, and Capterra India holds the top SERP slot for the head term.
6. **Not verified in Search Console.** I did not pull SpotAI's indexation; `scripts/gsc-index-status.py`
   covers the other four. Check it is added as a property before drawing conclusions from "0 ranked".

## Cross-site: what we are lagging on

The finding repeats on every one of the five sites:

- **Authority is the bottleneck, not content.** All five share one root domain with 15 referring
  domains and DA 4. The small competitors that beat us carry 36 to 166.
- **Competitors win with utility pages, not product pages.** Calculators (okfit), fee pages
  (mygymdesk), glossary and chat logs (streamscheme, instreamly), templates (FitRank niche),
  per-project pages (nevon). Our sites lean on product and blog pages, with few stand-alone tools.
- **Our rankings are mostly page two and below.** streamerOS has "obs tuning" at 1 and "visual
  tuning" at 21, but both have 50 to 90 searches; the 100-plus volume terms sit at 28 to 92.
- **No site has measurable brand search yet**, so unlike sensai.fit ("sens ai", 2,400) we cannot
  lean on the name.
