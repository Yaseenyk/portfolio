# Why DefendIt (MCA final year project) and the "crawled currently not indexed" post are not in the top 30

Date: 14 Nov 2026. Method: Google India results (treg, 21 queries, 30 results deep), site: checks, GSC saved data (analytics
dashboard), SE Ranking authority scores for our domains and the pages that rank, and a direct fetch of our pages and of
the pages that rank. treg spend: about $0.08 of the $0.50 allowed.

## What we measured

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Our pages in the top 30 for any of 15 MCA and "crawled currently not indexed" queries | 0 |
| DefendIt (campus.streamerosai.com), 3 months in GSC | 92 impressions, 1 click, average position 59.5 |
| Portfolio (yk.streamerosai.com), 3 months in GSC | 80 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 39.8 |
| Domain authority (SE Ranking domain inlink rank) | ours 14; ximi.ac.in 62; indiastudychannel 72; upgrad, oneindia, stanventures, onely 86 |
| DefendIt MCA guide indexed? | Yes (site: query finds it). It is indexed but ranks beyond 30. |
| "Crawled currently not indexed" post indexed? | **No.** GSC state: "Discovered, currently not indexed" (published 12 Nov). |

## Gap 1 (biggest): the post we want to rank is not in Google yet
It cannot rank for anything until it is crawled. It is two days old and has few internal links.

## Gap 2: authority and links, not content
The MCA guide is 2,385 words with 5 tables, an FAQ and structured data and a year in the title. That is more than ximi.ac.in
(931 words), indiastudychannel (599) and filemakr (433), which all rank. What those pages have that we lack is links and age.
Our domain scores 14 against 62 to 86. A guide written for a head term will not outrank them without links.

## Gap 3: the results are not a blog-vs-blog contest
- MCA queries: universities (ximi, rungta, sppuef, dypatiledu, onlinemanipal), upGrad, YouTube (about 5 of the top 12),
  Instagram, Scribd, Quora, Reddit, GitHub and programmatic geo pages (aislyn.in, authority 14 like us, ranks with
  thousands of city pages). A DefendIt-style guide competes with video, documents and forums for the same slots.
- "crawled currently not indexed": SEO agencies and Google's own help pages, authority 72 to 86.
  Long forms ("... next.js", "... opacity 0", "... blank page javascript") are won by nextjs.org, GitHub discussions,
  Reddit r/TechSEO and Google Search Central threads, i.e. community debugging threads, where a first-person fix fits.

## Gap 4: missing exact-match pages
- "mca final year project report format": filemakr ranks #2 with a 433-word page, authority 6. DefendIt has only a generic
  report-format guide. There is no MCA-specific report format, synopsis, or "source code" page.
- "mern stack project ideas for mca": GeeksforGeeks, appwars, collegeprojectexpert. DefendIt has a MERN project-ideas
  guide that is not yet in the top 30 and no MCA-specific angle in the title.

## Gap 5: the portfolio is nearly invisible for everything
80 impressions in three months. Its blog posts show at positions 25 to 75 for long-tail queries, with no head-term wins.

## Plan (in order of payoff)
1. Get the post indexed: request indexing in Search Console, link it from the home page and 3 related posts, share it
   in r/TechSEO, a GitHub discussion and the Next.js community with the fix.
2. Retitle it for the long tail people type: "Crawled - currently not indexed on a Next.js site: opacity 0 hid my page".
3. Build MCA-specific pages DefendIt lacks: MCA project report format, MCA synopsis, MCA projects with source code.
4. Earn links: a YouTube walkthrough per top project, free report templates on Scribd and SlideShare, answers on Quora and
   Reddit with the guide linked where it truly helps, college pages (/colleges/), and links from the portfolio.
5. Target long-tail first (authority matters less), then the head terms in 3 to 6 months.
6. Re-check positions on 28 Nov and 12 Dec with the same queries (about $0.03 each time).

Realistic expectation: the head terms will not reach the top 30 in weeks. Long-tail and the exact-match MCA pages can
move in 4 to 8 weeks if indexed and linked.
